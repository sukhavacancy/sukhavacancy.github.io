RRB ALP 2026 Notification: Apply Online for 11,127 Vacancies

Looking for a stable government job? The Indian Railways has released the highly anticipated RRB ALP 2026 indicative notification! If you're aspiring to become an Assistant Loco Pilot, this is your chance. The Railway Recruitment Board has announced a massive 11,127 vacancies for the RRB ALP Recruitment. Job seekers must apply online starting May 15, 2026. This notification is your first step toward an exciting railway career. Let’s dive into the complete details, from important dates and fees to the selection process, so you can easily prepare your application.

Important Dates & Post Details

Event / Detail Information
Name of the Post Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) Level-2
Total Vacancies (All Railways) 11,127
Opening Date of Application 15.05.2026
Closing Date for Submission 14.06.2026 (23:59 hrs)
Initial Pay Rs. 19,900/- (Pay Level 2)

Application Fees

While paying the application fee, keep the following structure in mind:

  • General / OBC / EWS: ₹500 (₹400 refundable after appearing in CBT 1)
  • SC / ST / PwD / Female / Transgender / Minorities / EBC: ₹250 (Fully refundable after appearing in CBT 1)

Age Limit

To be eligible for the Assistant Loco Pilot post, your age must be between 18 to 30 Years (as on 01.07.2026). Age relaxation for reserved categories applies as per government norms.

Qualifications

As per the official notification, specific qualifications will be fully detailed in CEN No. 01/2026. However, based on the standard ALP pattern, candidates must possess:

  • Matriculation (10th) / SSLC along with an ITI certificate or Course Completed Act Apprentices (CCAA).
  • Alternatively, a Diploma or Degree in specified Engineering disciplines is also accepted.
  • Medical Standard: Candidates must meet strict medical vision standards, including a 6/6 vision without glasses.

Selection Process

The selection process for RRB ALP involves multiple stages to test your technical knowledge and aptitude. Candidates must clear each stage to proceed:

  1. First Stage CBT (CBT 1): Qualifying in nature; features questions on Math, Reasoning, Science, and Awareness.
  2. Second Stage CBT (CBT 2): Consists of Part A (core subjects) and Part B (relevant trade knowledge).
  3. Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT): Also known as the Psycho test.
  4. Document Verification (DV): Verifying certificates and identity.
  5. Medical Examination: Strict vision and physical fitness tests.

Important Links

  • Official Apply Online Portal: https://www.rrbapply.gov.in

Other Important Information

Here are a few special instructions you must not ignore before applying:

  • Aadhar Verification: You are strictly advised to verify your primary details using your Aadhar card during the online application to avoid special detailed scrutiny and delays.
  • Match Your Details: Your Name and Date of Birth on Aadhar must match 100% with the details in your 10th class pass certificate.
  • Biometrics Update: Update your Aadhar with the latest photo and biometrics (Fingerprint & Iris) before applying.
  • Beware of Touts: Indian Railways urges candidates to be aware of brokers and job racketeers.

The online application portal will close on 14.06.2026 at 23:59 hrs.

There are a total of 11,127 vacancies available across all participating Railway Recruitment Boards.
